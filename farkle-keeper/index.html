Farkle Keeper support

Score keeper for Farkle played with real dice

Get help

Email support@pipandmerrystudios.com with your question, a bug, or a house rule you would like supported. Mention your iPhone model and iOS version if something is not working.

Common questions

I entered the wrong score. Tap Back to reopen the last turn, or tap a player's score to open the scorecard and tap any score to change it.

Can I type totals instead of tapping dice? Yes. Choose Keypad under Score entry on the home screen, or switch during a game from the menu at the top right.

How do I change the target score or the opening score? Set them under Rules on the home screen before starting a game.

Where are my games stored? Only on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, so deleting the app deletes its records.

Privacy

Farkle Keeper collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.
