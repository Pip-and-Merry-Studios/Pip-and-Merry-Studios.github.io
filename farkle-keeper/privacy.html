Farkle Keeper privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Nothing is collected

Farkle Keeper does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Everything stays on your iPhone

Player names, scores, past games and your settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent anywhere, and the app works without an internet connection.

Removing your data

Delete past games from Records inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it has saved.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, the updated policy will be posted here and inside the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions: support@pipandmerrystudios.com
